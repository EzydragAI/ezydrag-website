Construction

Built on the face.

  • {{i:tick}}Face F × F, corner radius F/4
  • {{i:tick}}Plate: same shape, offset F/8 down-left
  • {{i:tick}}Stroke F/20, always ink
  • {{i:tick}}Glyph: Inter 500 "e", half the face tall
  • {{i:tick}}Dot: radius 0.07F at (0.79F, 0.71F)
Clear space

Give it one plate of air.

Keep clear space equal to the plate offset (F/8) on every side. Nothing enters it.

Motion

Motion explains. It never decorates.

Linear timing. 0.3s for small things, 0.4s for headings, 0.6–0.9s for data moving. Anything longer than 5s gets a pause.

Client logos

Quiet, grey, the same size.

Their brand, our room. Every client and partner logo is shown in one ink grey on white, trimmed and optically scaled so none shouts louder than the rest.

  • {{i:tick}}One colour: ink grey #4D5163, transparent background. Never their colours on our pages.
  • {{i:tick}}Optical scale: height ≈ 1 / √(aspect ratio), adjusted for how dense the mark is. Wide wordmarks get shorter, square marks taller.
  • {{i:tick}}Equal cells: 180 × 64 px, 1px hairline, 12px radius. Logo centred, never cropped.
  • {{i:tick}}Always 70% opacity. No hover, no flip, no motion. Logos are proof, not buttons.
  • {{i:tick}}Only with written permission from the client.
