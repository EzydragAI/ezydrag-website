TL;DR

Atlassian starts charging for extra Rovo credits on 3 December. n8n bills AI in credits. Budget per outcome, not per token, or your agent will quietly run up the card.

What happened

So, I read pricing pages for fun. I know. I know. But stay with me, because this one matters.

On 1 September, Atlassian confirmed that from 3 December it "will enforce monthly allowances and charge for excess Rovo credits and Automation steps." Extra Rovo usage is $0.01 a credit. Extra automation is $0.50 per 1,000 steps.

Overage is switched on by default. Please read that sentence twice.

n8n was already billing its AI Assistant in credits based on tokens. Then n8n Agents gave us, honestly, the cleanest cost unit I've seen: "One turn with an agent is one execution."

Why you should care

For about a year, AI features felt free. They were never free. Now there's a meter, and a busy agent can run up a bill overnight while everyone is asleep, including, statistically, the person who set it up.

The sneaky part

If you turn overages off and the allowance runs out, new automations stop. And events missed during that pause aren't replayed. For a sales team, that's leads nobody follows up. Silently.

What we'd tell a founder

  • Price the outcome. Cost per lead followed up, per email checked, per report drafted. Not per token.
  • Set caps and alerts. Then decide what happens when you hit them. "Stop silently" is the worst possible answer.
  • Skip the model when you can. A known question with an approved answer doesn't need an AI call at all.
