2. Who this notice covers

This notice applies to:

  • Website visitors who browse www.ezydrag.com or www.ezydrag.in.
  • Prospective clients who build a project brief, email us, book a call or ask for a quote.
  • Client contacts such as the people at a client business we work with day to day.
  • Training participants who attend our AI training sessions.
  • People whose data appears in client systems that we work on during an engagement.

Our two roles

The law treats us differently depending on whose data it is.

  • When we decide why and how data is used (for example, your enquiry or your contact details as a client), we are a Data Fiduciary under the DPDPA and a controller under the GDPR.
  • When we process data on behalf of a client (for example, customer records inside a client's CRM that an agent we build reads), the client is the Data Fiduciary or controller and we are a Data Processor. We act only on the client's documented instructions, under a written agreement. If you are a customer of one of our clients, the client's own privacy notice governs your data, and you should contact them first.

4. How the project brief works

The project brief on our website runs in your browser.

  • Your answers stay in page memory while you fill it in. The page does not send them to our servers. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.
  • When you copy the brief, it goes to your device's clipboard. When you email it to us, your email provider delivers it and we receive it like any other email.
  • The brief does not use cookies or browser storage to keep your answers.

6. How we handle client data in AI engagements

Businesses trust us with access to their systems. These are our commitments for every engagement:

  • Instructions only. We process client data only to build, test, run and support the agreed workflow.
  • No model training on your data. We do not use client data to train or fine-tune AI models for anyone else. We choose AI model provider plans whose terms prohibit training on API inputs and outputs, and we use reduced or zero data retention options where the provider offers them.
  • Least access. We ask for the narrowest access each workflow needs, prefer read-only and scoped credentials, and remove access when the engagement ends.
  • Human review. Sensitive decisions, irreversible actions and uncertain outputs stay behind human approval, as agreed with you.
  • Sample data first. Where possible we prototype with sample or masked data before connecting live systems.
  • Written agreements. Data access, model providers, data location, retention and review controls are agreed in a data processing agreement or statement of work before sensitive data is connected.
  • Confidentiality. Everyone at EzyDrag who works on client data is bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Return and deletion. At the end of an engagement we return or delete client data, as the client chooses, and confirm deletion in writing on request, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising.

We share personal data only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as cloud hosting, email and productivity tools, video calling, accounting and invoicing. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • AI model providers that power the agents we build, when a workflow needs them. We list the providers used in each engagement in the client's agreement.
  • Voice and messaging partners, such as our partner Nutaan AI, when a workflow includes voice calls, WhatsApp, SMS, email or surveys with your customers. They process the data only to deliver those conversations.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and accountants, under confidentiality.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, such as a court order or a lawful request from a government agency.
  • A buyer or successor if EzyDrag is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under equivalent privacy protections. We will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different policy.

We keep a current list of our sub-processors and share it with clients on request. We tell clients before adding or replacing a sub-processor that handles their data, so they can object.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers and AI model providers store or process data outside India, for example in the United States or the European Union. The DPDPA allows transfers outside India except to countries the Government of India restricts. We will not transfer data to any restricted country.

For personal data covered by the GDPR, we transfer it outside the European Economic Area or the UK only with appropriate safeguards, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Clients can ask for data to stay in a particular region where the providers involved support it.

10. How we protect data

We use technical and organisational safeguards that suit the sensitivity of the data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where our providers support it
  • Multi-factor authentication and role-based access to our systems
  • Separate credentials and environments for each client
  • Logging and monitoring of access to client systems
  • Masking or minimising personal data in prompts and test sets where possible
  • Regular review of access and of our providers' security commitments
  • Confidentiality obligations for everyone who handles client data

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep the risk low and to act fast if something goes wrong.

11. If there is a data breach

If we become aware of a personal data breach:

  • Where we are a Data Fiduciary or controller, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals without delay, and send the Board a detailed report within 72 hours, as the DPDP Rules require.
  • Where the GDPR applies, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required.
  • Where we are a processor for a client, we will tell the client without undue delay and give them the information they need to meet their own obligations.
  • Cyber security incidents will be reported to CERT-In within six hours, as Indian law requires.

12. Your rights

Under the DPDPA (India)

You have the right to:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we process it
  • Know who we have shared it with
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Have your data erased when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it
  • Have your grievance addressed by us
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response

Under the GDPR (EU and UK)

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data in certain cases
  • Restrict or object to our processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

How to make a request

Email hello@ezydrag.in with the subject line "Privacy request". We may need to confirm your identity before acting. We do not charge for reasonable requests. We aim to respond within 30 days, and always within the time limits set by law. If we act as a processor for one of our clients, we will pass your request to that client and help them respond.
