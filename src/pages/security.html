Consent and compliance {{i:arrow}}
What is our data used for?

Only to run your agents. Scope and how long we keep it are agreed in writing before anything is connected.

Do the agents write to our CRM or marketing tools on their own?

No. Access starts read-only. Emails and changes wait for a named person to approve.

Which AI models do you use?

We use third-party language models. We name them for your project during scoping, so your team can review them.

Can we delete everything?

Yes. On request, or automatically when the project ends.
